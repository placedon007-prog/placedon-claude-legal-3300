The record, in motion — product walkthrough
01 · The record

Built for the record

Placedon reads the Companies Act, 2013, traces every answer to its source, and works inside the tools your compliance team already uses. Every output carries its citation and its operative date — or it abstains.

Connected to your filings

Built to read from MCA21 filings, board minutes, and your statutory registers — no re-keying, no copy-paste, no re-explaining the company each time you open a question.

Built for your obligations

Matrices for the duties the Act actually imposes: AGM timing under s.96, the board-meeting cadence under s.173, small-company status under s.2(85), and the Board's-report extracts.

Works where you file

Use Placedon in Word, in email, and alongside the secretarial software your team already runs. The citation travels with the answer wherever it goes.

Abstains by design

When a limb of a provision is undecided, Placedon says so and names the missing instrument. It never renders a fabricated figure to fill a gap.
